# Episode 21 · The Slow Billions (Warren Buffett's Thanksgiving 2026 gift)

Story: on Monday 16 Nov 2026 Warren Buffett, 96, converted 6,150 Class A Berkshire Hathaway shares into 9,225,000 Class B shares and gave them to four family foundations, about $5.5 billion, about four times his Thanksgiving 2025 gift. More than $160 billion of his Berkshire shares remain, and his plan (July 2026) sends them all to the same four foundations by 31 Dec 2034. His Thanksgiving letter says kindness "compounds, and at a better rate than anything Charlie and I ever bought". Angle: what happened + the mechanism (how one person ends up with that much to give: time and compounding). Feeling: awe.
