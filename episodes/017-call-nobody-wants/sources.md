## Facts and sources
1. The week to Fri 20 Nov 2026: TAIEX down 7,641.83 points, 17.5% (Next Apple, TWSE preliminary statistics, 21 Nov 2026, https://news.nextapple.com/finance/20261121/ACD20D356AA66E47B558957BADEA98B4; TVBS, 21 Nov 2026, https://news.tvbs.com.tw/money/4053813; Reuters via Business Recorder, 21 Nov 2026, https://www.brecorder.com/news/40450062/ai-selloff-drives-taiwan-stocks-to-worst-week-since-1990: "deepening its weekly loss to 17.5 percent, the steepest five-day rout since 1990").
2. Worst week in more than 30 years: Reuters "the steepest five-day rout since 1990" and The Edge (Reuters copy, 20 Nov 2026, https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/826619) "their worst week in more than three decades"; Bloomberg via The Star (23 Nov 2026, https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/11/23/taiwan-widens-short-sale-curbs-after-shares-dive) "worst weekly fall in 35 years"; Taiwanese press: "35 years" / "nearly 36 years". On screen and spoken: "more than 30 years".
3. $760 billion (the big number): the main board's (TWSE-listed) market value fell from NT$143.07 trillion (13 Nov) to NT$118.10 trillion (20 Nov), NT$24,966.509 billion or 17.45% (TWSE statistics via TVBS and Next Apple, above; Commercial Times, 22 Nov 2026, https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20261122700645-430201). At NT$32.936 per US$ (20 Nov close, Focus Taiwan via IntelliNews/Yahoo, 22 Nov 2026, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/taiwan-stocks-suffer-worst-week-192113134.html) that is about US$758 billion: "about $760 billion" (our conversion; footnote). Cross-check: Reuters/LSEG, "The index has lost US$1.12 trillion in value since Oct 6" (nearly 28%).
4. How margin works in Taiwan: for TWSE-listed stocks the broker lends up to 60% and the investor pays 40%; maintenance ratio = market value ÷ loan; a margin call when it falls below 130%, i.e. at 78% of the price (100 → 78, a fall of 22%, "about a fifth"); top up within 2 business days, otherwise the broker sells (forced sale, from T+3) (StockFeel, 22 Nov 2026, https://www.stockfeel.com.tw/融資-解釋-條件/; Pocket Securities, 7 Aug 2026, https://www.pocket.tw/school/articles/4247/; Business Weekly Smart, https://smart.businessweekly.com.tw/Reading/IndepArticle.aspx?id=6018893; TWSE notice via UDN, 23 Nov 2026: 2 business days to top up). "Yours now $18" = 78 − 60 (no fees or interest).
5. Record margin calls: 719 accounts on 18 Nov, 2,947 on 19 Nov, 6,462 accounts (NT$6.464 billion) on 20 Nov, records; forced sales on 20 Nov 632 accounts, NT$1.495 billion, also a record (BigGo Finance, TWSE dashboard data, 22-23 Nov 2026, https://finance.biggo.com/news/2bc0c628-5f14-46b8-9686-b41f318a6118 and https://finance.biggo.com/news/d5f1f1bb-c116-4221-b26e-885031125734); TVBS "逾6千戶遭追繳" and a record NT$63.7bn one-day cut in margin loans (https://news.tvbs.com.tw/money/4053795); LTN "追繳暴增…斷頭戶數、金額同創新高" (https://ec.ltn.com.tw/amp/article/breakingnews/5648265); FTV "追繳64億創新高" (https://finance.ftvnews.com.tw/news/detail/2026B23W0082). Bloomberg data via Lapaas Voice (23 Nov 2026): margin loans fell NT$49bn on 20 Nov, "the largest single-day fall in Bloomberg's data going back to 1996".
6. The loop (forced sales push prices lower, which sends more calls): The Edge (Reuters copy), Pepperstone's Dilin Wu: "add foreign selling and margin calls to the mix, and the decline can quickly feed on itself"; Goldman Sachs (via finwire/The Globe and Mail and Lapaas, 21-23 Nov): aggregate maintenance ratio about 132%, close to the 130% call line.
7. AI fears: Reuters (via Business Recorder/IntelliNews): worries over valuations, debt raised to build data centres, opposition to new ones, and the fallout from Jensen Huang's departure from Nvidia; CNA (21 Nov): "global volatility in AI-related stocks". TSMC is more than 40% of the market's value (CNA, 21 and 25 Nov 2026, https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611210005).
8. The line moved and calls cancelled: TWSE with the brokers lowered the minimum maintenance ratio from 130% to 120% from Tue 24 Nov; accounts below 130% that were sent calls before 24 Nov had the call records cancelled and no forced sale (CNA via UDN, 23 Nov 2026, https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9896495; China Times, 24 Nov 2026, https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20261124005146-260410; cnyes, 24 Nov 2026, https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6670997; finwire, 23 Nov: "Securities firms agreed to lower minimum margin maintenance ratios as of Tuesday to 120 per cent from 130 per cent, the exchange said"). At 120% the call comes at 72% of the price (on screen "$72"). Last time: 1998, from 140% to 120%, kept 17 years (China Times, cnyes). Also (not spoken): FSC short-selling curbs on 19 and 21 Nov, the first blanket ban since 2015 (CNA, https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611210013); the NT$500bn stabilization fund buying (CNA; Bloomberg via The Star).
9. Tue 24 Nov: TAIEX +3,310.46 points, +8.99%, to 40,122.35, the largest single-day point gain on record, after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang (Focus Taiwan, 25 Nov 2026, https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611250006; NOWnews, 25 Nov 2026, https://www.nownews.com/news/6895401; The Storm Media timeline, https://world.storm.mg/articles/1183981). Spoken: "jumped 9 percent"; on screen "+8.99% · biggest point gain on record".
10. Wed 25 Nov: fell as much as 3,440.45 points (8.57%) to 36,681.90, the second-largest intraday point loss on record, and closed at 38,195.43, down 1,926.92 points (4.80%) (CNA, https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202611250068.aspx; UDN, https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9901044; NOWnews, above; Al Jazeera, 25 Nov 2026, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/asias-stock-markets-dive-as-doubts-to-swirl-over-ai-boom: "plunging as much as 7.9 percent in morning trading").
