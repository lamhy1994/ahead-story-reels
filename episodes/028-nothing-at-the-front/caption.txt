Ever crawled through a traffic jam, then found nothing at the front? You might sell the same way.

The numbers: 22 cars on a 230 m track made a jam with nothing in the way (Sugiyama et al., New Journal of Physics, 2008). The stock market's 50 biggest days of 1946 to 1987 often came with no major news (Cutler, Poterba & Summers, 1989). The traffic in the video is a model, not advice.
