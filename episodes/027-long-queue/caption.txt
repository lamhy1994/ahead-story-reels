Ever picked the restaurant with the long queue, just because it had a queue? You might pick your trades the same way.

The numbers: once two people go in, the next one ignores her own hunch (Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer & Welch, 1992). In Abhijit Banerjee's famous example (Quarterly Journal of Economics, 1992), 99 of 100 people heard next door was better, and all 100 queued at the busy place. Models of how copying spreads, not advice.
