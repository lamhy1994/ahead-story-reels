**A fund share is backed by real bitcoin; outflows mean bitcoin leaving the fund, often sold** (sentences 4–6)
- The Block Research, "Bitcoin ETF Flows Explained: What ETF Inflows and Outflows Mean for Investors", 26 Jun 2026 (updated 14 Jul 2026): authorized participants return shares to the fund for "cash or bitcoin of equivalent value"; "When redemptions settle in cash, the fund typically sells bitcoin to fund the payout"; outflows lead to bitcoin sales "in many cases"; flows "do not dictate price". https://www.theblock.co/learn/406279/bitcoin-etf-flows-explained
- crypto.news (Olivia Stephanie), "How Bitcoin ETF flows work: Creations, redemptions, and reading the data", 6 Jul 2026: in-kind redemptions since July 2025; the dealer may hold, hedge or sell the coins "on its own schedule". https://crypto.news/how-bitcoin-etf-flows-work-creations-redemptions-explained/
- TFTC (20 Nov 2026): "the authorized participant process can trigger actual Bitcoin sales to satisfy redemptions". https://www.tftc.io/ibit-record-541m-outflow-bitcoin-etf-november-2026
