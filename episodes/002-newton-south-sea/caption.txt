In 1720, the man who explained gravity lost a fortune on one falling stock.

Isaac Newton sold his South Sea shares in April with a big profit. The price kept climbing, and in June he bought back in, close to the top. By December a share was worth less than in January.

Told from Newton's own letter (1724) and Andrew Odlyzko's study of Bank of England records. Historians still debate how much he really lost.
