In 1324, a king gave away so much gold that gold itself got cheaper.

Mansa Musa of Mali stopped in Cairo on his way to Mecca. Three months later the city was full of gold, and on the way home he had to borrow.

Told from 14th-century accounts (al-Umari, Ibn Khaldun). Historians still debate how big the price drop really was.
