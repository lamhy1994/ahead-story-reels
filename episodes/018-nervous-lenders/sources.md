## Facts and sources (each from 2+ independent sources; pages read 25 Nov 2026)
1. $466 billion (the big number): AI-related debt raised in Jan-Sep 2026, public bonds and private placements, more than four times the $101 billion of the same months of 2025; Morgan Stanley data. Reported by the FT (as cited by Coinpaper, 20 Oct 2026, https://coinpaper.com/37615/ai-companies-have-raised-466-billion-in-debt-this-year-who-pays-if-the-boom-slows, and Disruption Banking, 25 Nov 2026, https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2026/11/25/why-are-ai-credit-default-swaps-hitting-record-spreads/), IO Fund (23 Oct 2026, https://io-fund.com/ai-stocks/ai-debt-nears-500-billion-nvidia-oracle-coreweave: "Morgan Stanley data shows debt raised reaching $466 billion through September, up from $101 billion"), BingX/Coinpaper (3 Nov 2026). Not mixed with Goldman's $489bn to 22 July (IO Fund) or PIMCO/Goldman's ~$250bn of bond sales by the biggest AI companies (CNBC).
2. A credit default swap is insurance on a loan: a yearly fee per $100 of debt, paid out if the borrower can't pay. Reuters (25 Nov, via MIX-FM, https://mymixfm.com/2026/11/24/ai-credit-default-swap-spreads-widen/): "protection against the risk a bond issuer fails to meet its obligations", and "Oracle's record-high CDS spread of 356 bps reflects a cost $3.56 to insure $100 worth of Oracle debt"; The Next Web (25 Nov, https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-debt-fears-coreweave-bond-yield-oracle-nvidia-sell-off): "Credit default swaps insure against a default. A wider spread means investors see the debt as riskier." The $1 / $3 friend example is our illustration (labelled "your friend").

Oracle's record on Tue 24 Nov (US; "overnight" for Asia): five-year CDS 356 bps, a record (Reuters/S&P Global Market Intelligence, above; Reuters 25 Nov via KFGO, https://kfgo.com/2026/11/24/ai-credit-default-swap-spreads-widen/: "rose 17 bps to 356 bps"); CNBC via The Next Web: "rose 7% to 355.68"; Yahoo Finance/TIKR (24 Nov, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/not-good-oracle-credit-swaps-200031895.html): "Oracle's credit default swaps widened again on Nov. 24", hyperscaler swaps "record wide".
