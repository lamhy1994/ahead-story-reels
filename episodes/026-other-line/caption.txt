Ever switched lines at the supermarket, then watched your old line fly? You probably trade the same way.

The maths: with 3 equally fast lines, yours is the quickest 1 time in 3. A strategy that wins 6 trades in 10 still loses 3 in a row sooner or later (56% of the time within 20 trades, if each trade is independent: our own calculation). An illustration of chance, not advice.
