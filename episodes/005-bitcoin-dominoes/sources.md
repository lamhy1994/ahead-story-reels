## The picture example (not a fact about a real person)
- "Picture one of them, with $100. She borrows $900, and buys $1,000 of bitcoin. If the price falls 10%, her $100 is gone." Arithmetic: $1,000 × 10% = $100. In real trading the position is closed a little before a 10% fall (fees and the maintenance margin); the video keeps the round numbers and labels it "picture". "The lender sells her bitcoin for her" simplifies what a margin lender or a futures platform's liquidation engine does.
- "People who bought without borrowing still had every coin, just worth less": a description of how liquidation works (only borrowed positions are force-closed), not advice.
