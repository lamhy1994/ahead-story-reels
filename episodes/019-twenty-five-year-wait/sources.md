# Episode 19 · The 25-Year Wait (the November 2026 AI sell-off, compared with Cisco in the dot-com crash)

Story: the November 2026 AI stock sell-off (episodes 9, 10, 13, 14, 17 and 18 told index weighting, where "wiped-out" trillions go, Huang's return, the short sellers' worst day, Taiwan's margin calls and the debt behind the boom). New angle: how it compares with a past event. Cisco sold the routers and switches the internet ran on; on 27 Mar 2000 it became the world's most valuable company, priced at about 200 times its yearly profit; when the dot-com boom ended it lost roughly $400 billion of value and almost 90% of its share price by October 2002, while the internet (and Cisco's sales) kept growing; its share price only got back above the March 2000 record on 10 Dec 2025. Today Nvidia sells the chips the AI boom runs on, and is about 38% below its October 2026 record (25 Nov 2026). No forecast: "some say this time is different; the story isn't over yet".
